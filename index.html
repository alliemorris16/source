dolly parton

Dolly Parton is an American singer, songwriter, actress, philanthropist, and businesswoman, widely known as the "Queen of Country". Born in 1946 in Tennessee, she grew up in a poor farming family and began performing music at a young age. Her career spans over 60 years, with 50 studio albums and more than 100 million records sold worldwide. Parton is famous for songs like "Jolene," "I Will Always Love You," "Coat of Many Colors," and "9 to 5". Beyond music, she has acted in films such as 9 to 5 and Steel Magnolias, and she founded the Dollywood theme park and various philanthropic initiatives. Her work often blends country and pop music styles, and she is celebrated for her storytelling, business acumen, and charitable contributions.

  • Born January 19, 1946, in Sevier County, Tennessee — one of 12 kids
  • Country music icon — "Jolene," "9 to 5," "I Will Always Love You"
  • Wrote "I Will Always Love You," later a massive hit for Whitney Houston
  • Founded the Imagination Library (1995), mailing free books to kids worldwide
  • Owns Dollywood, her theme park in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee
  • Won multiple Grammys, plus a Rock & Roll Hall of Fame induction (2022)
  • Helped fund Moderna's COVID-19 vaccine research in 2020
  • Famous for her quote: "It costs a lot of money to look this cheap"
  • Has a statue of herself in front of the Sevier County courthouse
  • Turned down the Presidential Medal of Freedom twice (offered by two different presidents)
